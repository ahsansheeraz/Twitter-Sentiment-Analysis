How It Works

Welcome to the X Sentiment Analyzer! Here’s how to use the website:

Hashtag Analysis

Enter the hashtag in the hashtag field and specify the number of tweets you want to fetch in the second field. Click the "Analyze" button to submit your request. Within seconds, the tweets along with their sentiment will be displayed, accompanied by visualizations.
