About Us

Welcome to X Sentiment Analyzer, a cutting-edge tool designed to analyze sentiment on Twitter and provide users with insightful data on public opinions, trends, and emotions in real-time.

Our Mission

At X Sentiment Analyzer, our mission is to empower users with the ability to analyze sentiment across Twitter, enabling them to make informed decisions based on social media trends. Whether you're a business, researcher, or social media enthusiast, our tool helps you understand the emotional tone behind tweets, hashtags, and user accounts.

What We Do

We specialize in sentiment analysis, leveraging state-of-the-art natural language processing (NLP) techniques to classify tweets as positive, negative, or neutral. Our tool also allows you to explore emotions like happiness, anger, sadness, and surprise. With our intuitive interface, users can analyze individual tweets, specific Twitter accounts, or trending hashtags, giving them a comprehensive view of online sentiment.

Our Story

This project was developed as part of a final-year academic project by Ahsan Sheeraz, a computer science student. With a passion for data science and machine learning, Ahsan set out to create a simple yet powerful tool that would allow users to better understand public opinion through sentiment analysis. Through extensive research and coding, X Sentiment Analyzer was born—a platform that makes complex data accessible to everyone.

Technology We Use

We utilize the latest technologies in machine learning and natural language processing to deliver accurate sentiment results. Our platform is built using:

  • Python for backend development
  • Flask for building the web application
  • VADER for sentiment analysis
  • snScraper for fetching Twitter data
  • Seaborn and Matplotlib for data visualization
